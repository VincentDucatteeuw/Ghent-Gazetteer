Ghent city directories

The Wegwijzer der stad Gent is a yearly address book. Each volume lists the same inhabitants three times: the alphabetical list (name, occupation, address), the list per street (the residents of each street by house number) and the trade directory (name and address under each heading, such as Kruideniers, grocers). The scans are from Ghent University Library; the text was read automatically (OCR) and split into fields.

What is a person?

An observation is one line in one list, with the text as read and a crop of the scan. A person is a group of observations linked automatically as the same person, household or firm: on name, initial, street, house number and occupation (Splink, match probability ≥ 0.9). Someone listed under five trade headings has five observations in the trade directory. A link can be wrong. Two rows of the alphabetical list, a widow and someone else, or a firm and a person are never merged.

  • Linkage: hoog (high: every observation linked at ≥ 0.99), middel (medium: at least one weaker link) or niet gekoppeld (not linked: one observation).
  • To check: two rows in the list per street, different streets or house numbers within one person, or an observation the parser flagged. Such a person may have two addresses, or be two people.
  • Kind: organisatie for firms, brothers, sisters and institutions (schools, companies, asylums); otherwise persoon.

Streets and occupations

Streets are matched to the street list of the same volume (Namen der straten), with the French name, police district and canton. Spelling and OCR variants become one street; the printed spelling stays visible on each observation. A street not found in the street list says In de stratenlijst: nee.

Occupations are coded in HISCO (automatically, with OccCANINE) and grouped into major groups. The occupation as printed is shown next to it. In the trade directory the occupation is the heading the name is listed under.

Limitations

  • OCR makes mistakes, especially in names; when in doubt, compare with the crop or the scan (📖).
  • HISCO codes and street matches are automatic; a rare street can be matched to a similar one.
  • Persons are linked within a volume, not across volumes.
